# Disclaimer
This report is not all inclusive, nor it is intended to address the entirety of construction progress. It is concerned primarily with construction progress and is intended to provide near term management   information.   The   report   itself   serves   as   the   Agenda   for   the weekly/Biweekly/Monthly or as required progress meetings.

# Introduction
Purpose
This report is to be prepared by the construction contractor and submitted to the Construction Consultant and MODON Project Manager before 12:00 PM on every Wednesdays with Progress data cut off date is Tuesday. The premise behind this report and the key to successfully managing projects is to update schedules weekly.
Calendar
The complete report will serve as the agenda for the weekly site meetings. The Construction Manger will chair the meeting. Attendance shall be limited to essential personnel only.
Construction Contractor, appropriate Contractor staff, Construction Manager, Program Manager,
and MODON Project Manager shall be in attendance.
Minutes of Meeting will be taken by the Construction Manager and submitted to the Program Manager (If Applicable) within 48 hours. Minutes shall describe attendance, the conduct of the meeting, discussion items, and problem resolutions, and shall provide action items with responsible parties and due dates.
Construction Manager shall maintain the Action Items Register. At subsequent meetings, open
action items shall be discussed first.

# Weekly Progress Report Attachment
Weekly Progress Report Attachement
Contractor/Consultant	should  ensure  that	the	weekly  progress  report  are  supported  with
required attachment.

Those attachment should include the following as a minimum requirement:

Approved Primavera P6 Schedule in .xer format.
3-5 latest progress photographs in JPG format
Updated Primavera P6 schedule in .xer format & PDF Format Three weeks look ahead in PDF 	format. (as per plf link)
Remaining Works in PDF format.
Any other supporting document required per the development of the project.
