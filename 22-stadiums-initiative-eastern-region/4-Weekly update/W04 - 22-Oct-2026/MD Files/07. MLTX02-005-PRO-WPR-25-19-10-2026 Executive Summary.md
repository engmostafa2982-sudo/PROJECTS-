## Page 4

```text
              Introduction

Purpose
This report is to be prepared by the construction contractor and submitted to the Construction
Consultant and MODON Project Manager before 12:00 PM on every Wednesdays with Progress data
cut off date is Tuesday. The premise behind this report and the key to successfully managing projects
is to update schedules weekly.
Calendar
The complete report will serve as the agenda for the weekly site meetings. The Construction
Manger will chair the meeting. Attendance shall be limited to essential personnel only.
Construction Contractor, appropriate Contractor staff, Construction Manager, Program Manager,
and MODON Project Manager shall be in attendance.
Minutes of Meeting will be taken by the Construction Manager and submitted to the Program
Manager (If Applicable) within 48 hours. Minutes shall describe attendance, the conduct of the
meeting, discussion items, and problem resolutions, and shall provide action items with responsible
parties and due dates.
Construction Manager shall maintain the Action Items Register. At subsequent meetings, open
action items shall be discussed first.
```
