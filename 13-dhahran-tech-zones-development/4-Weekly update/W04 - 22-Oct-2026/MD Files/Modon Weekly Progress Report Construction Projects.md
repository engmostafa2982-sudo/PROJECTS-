# Disclaimer
This report is not all inclusive, nor it is intended to address the entirety of construction progress. It is concerned primarily with construction progress and is intended to provide near term management information. The report itself serves as the Agenda for the weekly/Biweekly/Monthly or as required progress meetings.

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
# Introduction

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
# Introduction
Purpose
This report is to be prepared by the construction contractor and submitted to the Construction Consultant and MODON Project Manager before 12:00 PM on every Wednesdays with Progress data cut off date is monday. The premise behind this report and the key to successfully managing projects is to update schedules weekly.
Calendar
The complete report will serve as the agenda for the weekly site meetings. The Construction Manger will chair the meeting. Attendance shall be limited to essential personnel only. Construction Contractor, appropriate Contractor staff, Construction Manager, Program Manager, and MODON Project Manager shall be in attendance.
Minutes of Meeting will be taken by the Construction Manager and submitted to the Program Manager (If Applicable) within 48 hours. Minutes shall describe attendance, the conduct of the meeting, discussion items, and problem resolutions, and shall provide action items with responsible parties and due dates.
Construction Manager shall maintain the Action Items Register. At subsequent meetings, open action items shall be discussed first.
